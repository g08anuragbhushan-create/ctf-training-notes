# 8-Week Training Plan

## Week 1 — Foundations & Tooling
Accounts (picoCTF, Cryptohack, OverTheWire, PortSwigger, HTB); toolkit (Python+pwntools, Burp, Ghidra, Wireshark, binwalk/steghide/zsteg/exiftool, gdb+pwndbg); intro to each domain; picoCTF General Skills; Bandit 0-10.

## Week 2 — Web: Injection & OWASP
SQLi (basic/UNION/blind), XSS (reflected/stored/DOM), command injection, OWASP Top 10. PortSwigger labs + picoCTF caas / get aHEAD. Cross-train: Cryptohack intro, Leviathan 0-3, forensics warm-up.

## Week 3 — Crypto: Classical & Symmetric
Classical ciphers, frequency analysis, XOR/OTP, AES ECB vs CBC. Cryptohack General/XOR/Block Ciphers. Cross-train: robots/logon, x86 intro, stego.

## Week 4 — Reverse Engineering
Assembly, calling conventions, Ghidra, gdb+pwndbg. Three crackmes + picoCTF RE. Cross-train: IDOR, Cryptopals Set 1, carving.

## Week 5 — Forensics
Carving, stego, PCAP, logs. picoCTF forensics + one PCAP lab. Cross-train: SSRF/XXE, hashes, patching.

## Week 6 — Web + Crypto Advanced
SSRF/XXE/JWT/deserialization; RSA/DH/modular arithmetic. PortSwigger advanced + Cryptohack RSA. Cross-train: 3-star crackme, pcap reconstruction.

## Week 7 — RE + Forensics Advanced
Buffer overflow/ret2win, canaries/ASLR, Android jadx; Volatility, disk images. pwn.college + Volatility. Cross-train: CORS, RSA attacks.

## Week 8 — Mock CTF & Gap Closure
4-hour timed mock CTF; gap analysis; write-up sprint; pre-competition checklist.

## Weekly rhythm
Monday session -> Tue-Thu practice -> Fri/Sat talk + 2 write-ups -> Sunday commit write-up + update tracker.
